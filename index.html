Meet the author

Kelly DaSilva

I’m a first-time children’s book author from Western Massachusetts and most importantly a mom who loves reading bedtime stories.

My journey into writing children’s books started in the simplest way: reading to my son. Those cozy moments of sharing stories, turning pages, and getting lost in imaginary worlds inspired me to create a story of my own. My son was the inspiration behind my first published book, and I’m so excited to share it with other families, little readers, and librarians.

When I’m not dreaming up stories, you can usually find me taking a walk with my family, building something out of LEGOs, listening to Taylor Swift, or wandering around an island in Animal Crossing. I also have two dogs, whom I affectionately refer to as my dodo birds. I believe children’s books have a special kind of magic. They can make kids laugh, spark their imaginations, create treasured memories, and give grown-ups an excuse to do funny voices while reading aloud.

Thank you for supporting a first-time author. I hope you’ll grab a copy, get cozy, and read along!

About the book

What happens when a young boy's curiosity meets a little dream magic? DakDak loves his stuffed dodo bird and can't stop daydreaming about the extinct creature. One night, he falls asleep while cuddling his favorite toy and dreams of meeting a real dodo bird. Together, they embark on a magical adventure through space, filled with wonder, friendship and surprises. This enchanting journey is perfect for young readers with curious minds by sparking imagination and the power of eartfelt friendship.
